## 3. DETAILED TECHNICAL & GAMEPLAY SPECIFICATIONS

### A. Deterministic 120 Hz Physics & Zero-Bug Core Loop
1. **Fixed Timestep Loop:** Run physics strictly at `FIXED_DT = 1 / 120` seconds using an accumulator pattern. Clamp incoming `requestAnimationFrame` delta to `<= 0.05` seconds (50 ms) to prevent spiral-of-death physics explosions after tab switches or ad pauses.
2. **Continuous Collision Detection (CCD):** Sub-step high-speed projectiles whenever frame displacement exceeds `0.4 * projectile.radius`. Resolve circle-vs-rounded-AABB (blocks, walls) and circle-vs-circle (bumpers) accurately so projectiles **never** tunnel through walls, skip blocks, or clip out of bounds.
3. **Anti-Softlock Safeguards (Mandatory):**
   - Detect near-horizontal trapping (`|vy| < threshold` across consecutive wall bounces) and apply a subtle downward gravity/deflection impulse.
   - Track elapsed time since last block hit or downward progress; trigger an automatic fast-recall / run-completion sequence if a projectile stalls.
   - Include a clean player-accessible **Fast-Forward (2x)** or **Recall** button if a shot lingers past a threshold, matching the GDD.
4. **Controls & Aiming:**
   - Support both mouse drag-and-release and single-finger touch drag-and-release seamlessly via Pointer Events.
   - Render a crisp, physics-accurate dotted trajectory preview (including first-wall/block reflection preview) while aiming.
   - Allow canceling a shot by dragging back to the origin/deadzone without wasting the launch.
   - Prevent all browser interference: `touch-action: none`, `user-select: none`, disable right-click context menu on canvas, and `preventDefault()` on Space/Arrow keys so portal pages never scroll accidentally.

### B. Muted-Friendly Visual Polish ("Juice") & High-CTR Aesthetic
Every visual detail must look kinetic, crisp, and immediately readable even with audio completely off:
- **Zero Intro Walls:** After minimal fast initialization (< 1 second), drop a new player directly into playable Run 1 with a subtle animated drag-and-release hand cue over the launcher. Dismiss the cue immediately on first drag.
- **Tactile Feedback:**
  - Micro hit-stop frames (20–50 ms freeze) on TNT chain detonations, Vault breaks, and critical hits.
  - Directional camera shake proportional to impact force (clamped so it never induces nausea).
  - Progressive multi-stage crack overlays and white hit-flash frames on damaged ore blocks.
  - Floating coin popups and kinetic coin magnets flying toward the HUD counter.
  - Distinct color-coded biome strata (matching the exact biomes, names, depths, and multipliers in `ricochet_miner_game_design_document.md`) with smooth vertical camera tracking as the sawblade smashes deeper.
  - **Resolved Biome & Boss Gate Mechanics:**
    - *Bedrock Boss Gate:* 3 distinct weak-point cores with persistent HP embedded in a 9-column barrier; direct hits on a core deal 100% damage, while hits on the armored barrier bounce off with metallic sparks and transfer 20% splash damage to the nearest surviving core. Breaking all 3 cores shatters the gate and unlocks the next biome's Elevator Checkpoint.
    - *Subzero Abyss Frost Blocks:* Slide 1 tile horizontally away from impact if that adjacent tile is empty.
    - *Cyber Neon Vault Warp Portals:* Strictly one-way (entry to exit 1.5–2 chunks deeper) with a 0.4s re-entry cooldown, +2 bonus bounces, +30% exit speed boost, and downward ejection angle.
- **Typography & Contrast:** Bold, high-contrast geometric typography rendered with crisp outlines/drop shadows so numbers remain legible on small 360px mobile screens and 1080p desktops alike.

### D. Unified Multi-Portal SDK Bridge & Ad Compliance
Implement `PlatformSDK.ts` as a rock-solid abstraction layer driven by `import.meta.env.VITE_PLATFORM` (with automatic runtime host detection fallback when appropriate):

   - After ANY ad callback (`adFinished`, `adError`, adblock, or timeout failsafe): restore audio state (respecting user's manual mute toggle!), unlock inputs, and resume cleanly.
